Map editing storyboard

Editing is a mode you step into from the map, not a set of tools that are always on. It keeps the everyday map calm for people who only look, and makes it obvious when a tap will change something.

  • Each change saves on its own

    No draft to publish later. Every save shows an Undo for ten seconds.

  • Place with a crosshair or GPS

    A finger hides what it taps. The map moves under a fixed cross instead, or you stand at the pole and use your location.

  • Snap and connect

    Points snap onto lines and line ends snap onto points. Snapping creates the connection, so nobody fills in a "connected to" field by hand.

  • Remove means archive

    Archived assets leave the map but keep their history and readings. Permanent delete is a separate admin action.
