Farm map overhaul samples

Static prototypes for views and infrastructure on the farm map. The paddock outlines and soil pH are real. Every asset is made up and placed by hand around the Woodcote paddocks, and live readings are simulated. The research behind them is in docs/map-overhaul/README.md.

  • Map views

    The map with Soil, Irrigation, Infrastructure and Property views. Tap a pivot, pump or pole to see its card and what it is connected to. Try the search with "4812" or "pump", and narrow the window to see the phone layout.

    Decide: are these the right four views? Does tapping a pivot and seeing its water supply light up feel useful?

  • Icon sheet

    Every asset glyph, the status and selection states, line and area styles, and what shows at each zoom level.

    Decide: neutral badges with a status ring (A), or badges coloured by type (B)? Any glyph you can't read at a glance?

  • Editing storyboard

    Adding a pole, drawing a water main, editing a pivot on a desktop, removing and moving. Seven frames, not interactive.

    Decide: should everyone who can open the site be able to edit, or only some people?
